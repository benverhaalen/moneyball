# Player evidence to a roster decision

Use deep session reasoning first. Do not require a new forecasting experiment before using a defensible conditional judgment. Keep empirical superiority unclaimed until measured. This is one Moneyball workflow, sharing the connected service, evidence store and exact-rule accounting.

## Normal journey

1. Read `league_context` and saved `league_strategy`. Refresh rules, squads, availability and period forecasts separately before actionable advice. Structural strategy is reused until its binding changes.
2. Call `player_assessment_context` for up to twelve exact provider IDs; it includes every team's holdings, selected player evidence, forecasts, prior assessments, source dates and retained research references. Optional pipeline datasets reuse the existing warehouse. Repeat bounded packets for a larger cohort.
3. Reason across ability, opportunity, availability, environment, development, contracts, market and roster fit. Do not substitute eight checkmarks for reasoning. Identify the useful NFL job, employment path, valuable work, competition, execution, availability, and legal-lineup alternatives. Missing facets remain missing.
4. Save a claim graph with `save_player_assessment`. Each claim separates observation/interpretation/assumption/forecast, confidence, intended use, rival explanation, dependencies and reversal condition. Sources have dates, scope and origin groups. Several websites repeating one report are not independent corroboration. A reference's reputation or complexity does not validate a player forecast.
5. Call `compare_assessment_worlds` with explicit current/future worlds, a hold alternative and complete legal trade packages. Each projected player has a claim driver and either scoring components or team-volume/share inputs. Supply availability and every scoring component, including explicit zeros; missing inputs never silently become zero. All teams face the same world. Compare actual substitutions, displaced bench players and required drops, not player totals alone.
6. Explain the decision in this session. State the preferred action at the obtainable price, when it loses to holding, present sacrifice versus future benefit, bargaining feasibility and the biggest unresolved assumption. Confidence is separate. Do not call a representative-week lineup delta a season expectation or championship probability.
7. Reopen assessments instead of rederiving them. Refresh facts that could reverse the action; revise stale bindings. Disable claims/mechanisms to inspect dependence while retaining the original record. Do not remove unconventional evidence simply because it lacks a ready scalar model.

## Judgment before calibration

`rely` means host-selected evidence can drive arithmetic. It does not mean independently proven. `conditional` also permits arithmetic but requires its condition to remain visible. `explore` preserves a lead without using it as a numeric driver. `set_aside` preserves rejected evidence and its reason. High/medium/low/unknown confidence is a separate field; do not mechanically map those labels to probabilities.

Ask what would have produced the same observation under a rival explanation. A rising target share might reflect ability, an absent teammate, a new scheme, a small denominator, or defenses conceding low-value catches. Model the path relevant to the decision rather than extrapolating the slope. Distinguish skill durability from job security; contract exit from vacancy; vacancy from inherited usage; usage from value in the actual scoring and lineup.

Use different measurement biases to triangulate a claim. Role snapshots, untargeted execution, conversion results, contracts, and reporter observations answer different questions. Preserve contradictory evidence. A source that is unsuitable for a numerical forecast may still identify a scenario worth retaining. For coaching/environment changes, separate continuity, personnel turnover and scheme turnover; their effects need not move in the same direction.

## References and numeric scope

Read `moneyball://research/repositories` for retained repositories, specific transfer jobs, limitations, rights and integration status. The catalog is not an installed model ensemble. No third-party restricted code or data is bundled.

The calculator supports explicit shrinkage, supplied Markov transitions, volume/share/conversion decomposition and zero-if-absent availability mixtures. Parameters remain author assumptions unless separately evidenced. It is not a fitted HSMM, injury-risk model or causal-effect estimator. Variance is not summed across teammates. These calculators can inform scenarios; their outputs do not automatically become projection drivers.

Future worlds currently hold roster membership, IR/taxi and eligibility fixed except explicit trades/drops. No future acquisition, retirement, rookie intake, keeper cost or counterparty consent policy is inferred. Managed lineups only; best-ball needs joint outcomes. Current player status is not carried into another period as a permanent forecast. Redraft cannot retain its roster into next year; keeper futures need retention costs modeled before evaluation. New assumptions cannot be backdated for historical evaluation.

Use existing waiver/lineup/trade tools for acquisition mechanics. There is no new universal player rating or automatic trade instruction. The calling session remains responsible for judgment and the championship objective.
