# Experimental mock-draft laboratory

The laboratory supports controlled draft experiments and durable decision records. Its older fixture targets a twelve-team, 28-round snake with a specific ten-slot starting lineup. This is an experimental format, not a claim of general support for every connected league.

Use synthetic boards for development. A browser-driven mock requires explicit authorization for the account and the specific room. Bind the run to the observed league/draft/team identity before any action; never authorize a room merely because it is absent from an old real-draft blacklist. Keep browser sessions and output stores private and separate. The connected public agent tools do not submit picks.

Record the board, evidence cutoff, complete alternatives, decisive assumptions and recommendation before a timed choice. Preserve failed runs in the denominator. A bot selection pattern is not calibrated human availability or football value. Account/platform interference is a possible experiment confound; verify concurrent operation instead of assuming isolated tabs imply isolated drafts.

Measure timeliness, legality, rule consistency and evidence support separately from eventual results. Championship percentages from a simulator are conditional on supplied forecasts, opponent behavior and future-management assumptions. They are not calibrated success probabilities. See [draft context](draft-context.md), [live driver research](live-draft-driver.md) and [analytics](analytics.md) for narrower mechanisms and limitations.
